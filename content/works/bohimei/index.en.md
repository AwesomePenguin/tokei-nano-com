At first, every tomorrow feels much like the day before. Then the years gather, and the tape player no longer turns.

Built around piano, strings and electric guitar, “墓碑銘” follows the distance between a remembered youth and the person looking back.

“また明日” (see you tomorrow), once an easy promise between friends, becomes a line left in an old diary. One tomorrow after another settles like snow, leading into a boundless white plain.
